Chapter 1

The floor under identity

G10 found that every external identifier in the graph could be replaced with a fabricated one and all nine competency questions still passed. That is not a testing oversight. It is a predictable consequence of treating identity as a value rather than as a cost, and there is a theorem that says so in advance.

★★★ load-bearing 12 min G10 · v0's error class this is the one that already bit
Provenance. This chapter rests on unpublished single-author work (On the Necessary Substructures of Finite Contact Graphs; The Minimum Analytic Finite Graph). It has not been peer-reviewed and nobody else has checked it. Every engineering claim below is stated so that it stands on its own: if you reject the theory entirely, the proposed gate is still a gate, and the argument for it is still an argument. The theorem is offered as the reason the gate is the right shape, not as evidence that it works.

1. What was actually observed

From GAPS_2.md G10 — a deliberate reproduction of the v0 defect. All fifteen skos:exactMatch links in the serialised graph were repointed at a ChEBI identifier that does not exist, and every automated gate was re-run:

Three independent gates, all green, all blind. And they are blind for three different reasons, which is what makes this a class of failure rather than a bug:

GateWhat it checksWhy it misses
Competency questions Labels match expected labels Label and identifier come from the same reference_data.py row — a common-mode failure. Both are wrong together.
SHACL shape The PURL has the right shape CHEBI_99999999 is perfectly well-formed. Shape is not reference.
URIRef test The link object has the right type Added in G0 to fix string-vs-IRI. A fabricated IRI is still an IRI.
The shape of the failure

Each gate checks a local property of the link — its spelling, its type, its agreement with a label drawn from the same source. No gate checks the one thing that makes an identifier an identifier: that something outside this graph answers to it. That is not an accident of which tests were written. It is what happens when identity is modelled as a value a triple carries rather than as a relation to something the project does not own.

There is a name worth having for the state G10 revealed, because the ordinary words are all wrong for it. The system was not mistaken — a mistaken system holds a false belief, and this one held no belief about denotation at all. It was not uncertain — uncertainty is a quantity a system can report, and nothing here had a channel to report it on. The accurate description is epistemic blindness: a condition in which a system lacks the apparatus to represent a question, and therefore cannot distinguish between having answered it and never having asked.

Why the word earns its place

Blindness is not a low confidence score; it is the absence of the axis along which confidence would be measured. This matters practically, because the two conditions have opposite remedies. Uncertainty is reduced by gathering more of the same kind of evidence — more tests, more rows, tighter thresholds. Blindness is not reduced by any of that: running the nine competency questions a thousand more times over the corrupted graph returns nine thousand passes. It is remedied only by adding an instrument that can see the missing axis, which in G10's case means comparing against the authority itself. Every proposal in this series is an instrument for one such axis, and Chapter 9 §3 argues that the corruption run which exposed this one should be a permanent test rather than a finding in a document.

2. The theorem that predicts it

The individuation papers begin from a premise about wholes rather than about graphs: a non-completable medium is one no finite process exhausts. Not "infinite" in any cardinality sense — an order condition only, that there is no terminal stage. ChEBI is non-completable in exactly this sense: there is always another release, another cross-reference, another isobaric species not yet distinguished.

Floor from non-completability Contact Graphs, Thm 2.6 · Minimum Analytic Finite Graph, Thm 3.1

Let the medium be non-completable and let v be a proper part of it. Then the identification of v cannot be completed at any finite stage, and its identification residual is strictly positive: ρ(v) > 0.

Proof sketch: suppose the identification were complete. Completion means every part of the medium has been brought to bear in distinguishing v from the rest — so the comparison exhausts the medium. That contradicts non-completability, which guarantees a further stage containing something not yet consulted. Hence the residual never reaches zero.

The corollary is the part that matters here. Because no identification completes, the boundary content separating any one thing from the rest is bounded below by a positive constant β — the floor. And a floor that is positive means:

Identity is a region, never a point

An identifier does not denote a point. It names a cut of positive weight — the region of boundary by which a thing is told from everything else. Two consequences follow immediately, and both are already visible in this repository: a graph can carry a well-formed identifier that denotes nothing, because well-formedness is a property of the string and denotation is a property of the cut; and no amount of internal checking closes that gap, because the cut is against material the graph does not contain.

3. Why this is the right diagnosis and not a retrofit

A theory that merely re-describes a bug you already found is worth nothing. The test is whether it predicts something you did not already know. Three checks:

Theorem says

Measurement is reshuffling: it conserves the medium, so it tightens a thing's boundary from outside but never delivers its interior. Adding checks reduces exterior ambiguity and cannot reach identity.

Repository shows

G0 added the URIRef test; G2 added the SHACL PURL shape. Both tightened the boundary. Neither could detect a wrong-but-well-formed identifier — the interior was never touched. Predicted, not observed after the fact.

Theorem says

Individuation is by negation: a thing is fixed only as the complement of what it is not. Any determination that names a distinguished member regresses.

Repository shows

RATIONALE.md §3.2 — typing molecules as substrates or products "is wrong before it starts", because E. coli AlaA carries the same Rhea master as human ALT1 with roles reversed. Roles are relational, not intrinsic. That is negation, arrived at from the chemistry.

Theorem says

Point-identity is refuted two independent ways: resolution never saturates, and independent lines of measurement never become redundant.

Repository shows

ChEBI microspecies. L-glutamate(1−) ≠ L-glutamic acid; the distinction only exists because someone built an instrument fine enough to need it. Each generation of resolving power splits an identifier that used to be one thing.

The strongest form of the argument

If a chemical identity were a point, one sufficiently precise instrument would fix it permanently, and no finer one would ever be wanted. The existence of mass spectrometry, then high-resolution MS, then ion mobility — each generation distinguishing species the last could not — is standing evidence that identity is a region. You cannot both believe that analytical chemistry has a future and believe that an identifier denotes a point.

4. What this proposes building

G10 was closed by adding CQ10 and CQ11 — competency questions that project the ChEBI and Rhea PURLs themselves, so label and identifier must agree. That was the right immediate fix and it is already in the repository. What the theorem adds is the observation that CQ10 still compares the graph against itself: the expected set is duplicated from reference_data.py, so a wrong identifier entered consistently in both places passes. GAPS_2.md says this explicitly — "the second place is a review checkpoint" — and a review checkpoint is a human, not a gate.

4.1 A dereference gate

The missing gate is the one that leaves the graph. For every skos:exactMatch object, fetch the identifier from its authority and assert that what comes back agrees with what we claim locally — not that something comes back.

Run in CI as a network-marked, non-blocking job — the same category as the two existing live federated tests. Non-blocking matters: an outage at ChEBI must not fail an unrelated merge request. But it produces an artefact, and a red artefact is loud in a way a passing suite is not.

Why this catches exactly v0's error and CQ10 does not

v0's failure was systematic: reference.py resolved ChEBI via KEGG's conv/chebi cross-reference, and that mapping returns the neutral parent. Every identifier was off by exactly one hydrogen per unit of charge — internally consistent, uniformly wrong. A label oracle cannot see it. CQ10 cannot see it, because the expected PURLs would have been copied from the same wrong source. A formula-and-charge comparison against ChEBI itself sees it on the first entity: CHEBI:16015 reports C5H9NO4 charge 0, while the graph claims charge −1.

4.2 Recording the floor rather than pretending to close it

The theorem says the gate above cannot be completed either — dereferencing checks the boundary against one authority at one moment, and the medium keeps growing. The honest response is not to add gates until the residual looks like zero; it is to record the residual as a first-class property of the dataset.

tacat:notCompared is the unusual field and it is the one the theorem argues for. A dataset that states what it did not check is making a stronger claim than one that reports a green tick, because it has drawn a boundary a reader can test.

5. What this does not fix

Be able to say this out loud
  • A dereference gate is still a boundary check. It compares two sources; it does not establish that either is right. If ChEBI itself carries a wrong formula, both agree and both are wrong. The theorem says this is not fixable, only bounded — which is why the proposal is a recorded residual and not a certificate.
  • It introduces a network dependency into the definition of correctness. That is a real cost. The mitigation — non-blocking, cached, artefact-producing — is a compromise, and the alternative (vendoring a ChEBI snapshot and checking against that) trades freshness for determinism. Both are defensible; the choice should be recorded in DECISIONS.md rather than made silently.
  • The theory is unpublished. The gate stands without it. If someone asks "why should I believe your floor theorem?", the answer is that you need not — the deliberate corruption in G10 is the empirical case, and it is reproducible in this repository today.
