06 · The languages

Four languages, one graph, and a converter that refuses.

Each language serves a domain that a general-purpose language makes awkward, and each emits the same kind of thing: facts on nodes of one graph. They are not libraries with a shared data format — they are separate surface syntaxes over one semantics, which is why a circuit result and a mass-spectrometry acquisition can occupy the same node without either knowing about the other.

The composition, in one sentence

A question is posed in whichever language states it naturally; the runtime dispatches to whichever modules can answer; each writes its answer as a fact keyed fact:<module>#<chunk> onto the node named. No language has to know what the others can do — only the runtime does, and it does not translate between them. It keeps their outputs whole.

Eighteen modules are registered in the running system. Four of them are the languages below; the rest are contributors — a virtual mass spectrometer, a memory kernel, a remote-dispatch pool, and the cytochrome P450 monograph itself as a source of facts.

The common thread

Three of the four declare a floor — a resolution below which distinctions are not expressible — and enforce it at compile time. In honjo, floor must be > 0; the sharp cut is not expressible is a type error. In graffiti, a program with no declared floor does not compile. The floor is idea 02 →

honjo · .hj — one verb, and the verb is cut

The cheminformatics language. Its single computational primitive is the cut: individuating a part from a bounded whole at a strictly positive irreducible cost. The semantic identity it carries is that generating an atom, forming a bond, and tracking an item through a process are not distinct operations — they are the same cut at different arities.

ArityThe cut isIn chemistry
1individuating one item from the mediuman atom
2a cut between two itemsa bond

So the language has one verb and one composition rule, and atom generation, compound construction, molecular geometry and causal process tracking all follow rather than being separate features. The cut is the individuation idea →

Accountability is in the type system

Every value carries the floor at which it was cut and the provenance of the data it was cut from. A value claiming zero residue is rejected at compile time, and a value computed over supplied data can never be mistaken for one computed over stated data. Evaluation is measurement: the committed-cut count is monotone, so every program carries an intrinsic clock and an intrinsic audit trail.

SBS · .sbs — systems biology shaders

A network language: declare nodes and weighted edges, then observe, perturb or navigate. It compiles to GLSL and solves on the graphics hardware, with a CPU fallback. The reason it exists as a language rather than a library is that the interesting operation is perturbation — inhibit this edge by this factor, and tell me what moved — which is clumsy to express as function calls and natural as a declaration.

This is the circuit reading of a metabolic network →

shapeshifter · .ss — virtual mass spectrometry

Declares an objective, an instrument, a sequence of phase blocks and a validate condition. It compiles a stage and executes it against a capability layer, so the program says what is to be acquired and what would count as success rather than which library calls to make in which order. The instrument's own resolution limits enter here. What an instrument entitles you to conclude →

The runtime then forward-simulates the experiment — no instrument present. classes: ["PC"], mz_window: [150,500] expands to a cross-product over chain lengths, double-bond counts, adducts and polarity: thousands of predicted ions from four lines. And the validate block does real physics, deriving a minimum transient duration from the mass–energy–time uncertainty relation, so you can assert an instrument setting is physically achievable before spending the run.

graffiti · .grf — search as individuation

Not “find a document” but: separate a claim from an undifferentiated medium, at a stated resolution floor, and either converge on one claim-region or decline honestly. A seek declares what it excludes (not{…}), what it moves toward, which catalysts to compose, and what to do if the evidence splits.

It makes “I don't know” a typed value

A search returning three mutually incompatible answers normally either picks one — silently wrong — or throws, losing the information. Graffiti returns a Decline carrying the actual competing claim-regions. And a seek that reaches contested closure without an otherwise decline clause is a runtime refusal: the interpreter will not guess. Zero exclusion criteria is likewise an error — “individuation is undefined”.

The converter: what a format can state, and what it merely implies

Structures do not enter the chemistry language directly — they arrive through a converter, and the converter is where the honesty lives. Read CCO with any toolkit and you get a molecule with nine atoms. Six of those hydrogens were never in the file: a valence model invented them. In the output object, and everywhere downstream, they are indistinguishable from six hydrogens someone actually recorded.

The claim

That indistinguishability, not the conversion difficulty, is the substantive problem. Conversion is largely solved; the audit of conversion is not. When two pipelines agree you cannot tell whether they agree because they read the same facts or because they applied the same convention to the same silence.

Scope, stated precisely. This is a capability-typed, refusal-first source-description discipline for chemical formats, in the lineage of capability-aware query planning, with element-level provenance. It is not SPARQL federation, ontology alignment, OWL reasoning, or an SBML/BioPAX bridge — a plan may name several sources of different formats and read from each, and that is the whole of what "federated" means here.

Provenance typing — the number that should worry a curator

Every vertex, edge and attribute in a converted graph carries a tag recording whether the source stated it, the converter supplied it from a declared convention, or it is absent. Provenance is preserved under composition of conversions, and the supplied fraction is computable without re-reading the source.

Measured over a 48-structure corpus in two formats

A mean of 73.4% of the elements of a SMILES-derived contact graph are supplied rather than stated, with no structure below 50%. The majority of a translated structure is convention, not record — and today a downstream analysis cannot tell a hydrogen the file recorded from one a valence model invented.

Capability declaration — refusing before reading

Each source format is characterised by the set of structural features it can state. Translation is total and faithful exactly on the requests whose feature requirements the format's capability set contains — which makes a large class of translation failures decidable statically, before any record is read.

Measured: capability containment decided 19 of 24 format–request pairs before reading anything, and agreed with the post-read outcome on all 24.

Two faithful records, one molecule, undetected disagreement

This is the sharpest consequence, and it is checkable in a minute. Take benzene and write it two ways — both valid SMILES, both faithful to what they state: c1ccccc1 with a lower-case aromatic ring, and C1=CC=CC=C1 with an explicit Kekulé assignment.

InstrumentVerdict
Molecular formulaidentical — C₆H₆ both
Canonical InChIidentical — by design, it normalises bond-order assignment away
Round-trip testpasses — it compares a structure with itself, not two representations
Contact graphdiffers — 6 delocalised contacts against 0, and per-contact cell counts against none

And the supplied fraction moves in exactly the right direction: the aromatic form is φ = 0.750, the Kekulé form 0.625, because stating per-contact cell counts removes them from what convention must supply. The gap is a measurement of how much the aromatic notation leaves to the reader.

Neither graph converts into the other without supplying a fact. The ring commits nine cells; exactly two integer assignments sum to nine — the two Kekulé forms — and the source does not choose between them. Assigning 1.5 to each bond does not help: that asserts a pairwise claim where the source made a ring-level one.

Measured over ten aromatic molecules: contact graphs differ 10/10, molecular formula agrees 10/10, detected by formula-level comparison 0/10.

That 10/10 is not a base rate. The ten pairs were constructed to contain an aromatic ring, so the mechanism was guaranteed present. What the run establishes is that wherever it is present it is invisible to formula-level and canonical-identity comparison — not how often it arises. A base rate would need a corpus not selected for the mechanism, and that does not exist here.

Verdicts, not empty values

A translation returns a labelled verdict rather than a graph that may or may not be trustworthy. The argument for this is a theorem: a converter with a graph-or-nothing interface conflates at least four distinguishable outcomes, and no discipline on its callers can recover them.

What actually happenedWhat a null return says
The format cannot state this feature at allnull
The record is silent on it, though the format could state itnull
The record states it and the converter could not parse itnull
The converter succeeded and the answer is genuinely emptynull

These are four different situations for a curator, and a pipeline that returns null for all of them has destroyed the distinction before anyone can act on it. This is the same discipline as refusing rather than answering →

“How much of my data was invented?” as a precondition

The plan language makes the supplied fraction a declarable, statically checked precondition of an analysis rather than something nobody computes:

Refusal has a cost, and the paper says so

Pipelines that today proceed on repaired structures will instead halt, and an analyst has to decide what to do. Surfacing that decision is the point — but for an automated pipeline it is a real cost, not a free improvement.

What the operating system does

The runtime's job is deliberately small, and the smallness is the design. It does not know chemistry, does not compare results to expectations, and does not schedule.

The OS doesThe OS does not
Execute every chunk bundled on a nodeChoose among chunks
Route emitted values onto nodes as keyed factsInterpret what a value means
Record which values were read to produce whichOrder the graph in advance
Convert a raised exception into a valueHalt, or issue a verdict
Maintain a monotone commitment countCompare a result to an expectation

Because the runtime judges nothing, the languages can disagree without the system breaking: two modules writing different facts about one node is the normal case, not an error to resolve. Adjudication belongs to a consumer that reads both. How the runtime executes →
